### Group Drop 3: The 2 Number Systems in Korean (Quick Lesson Drop)
```text
Mini Korean Lesson of the day for the group! 💡🇰🇷

Why does Korean have TWO number systems?
1️⃣ SINO-KOREAN (일, 이, 삼, 사...): Used for MONEY (Won/Naira), DATES (Year/Month), and MINUTES.
Example: 10,000 Won = 만원 (Man-won).

2️⃣ NATIVE KOREAN (하나, 둘, 셋, 넷...): Used for COUNTING ITEMS (개), PEOPLE (명), and HOURS (시) on the clock.
Example: 2 People = 두 명 (Du-myeong).

Never say "이 명" for two people — that sounds like "Two names" or "Tooth people" to a native Korean! 😂
```
