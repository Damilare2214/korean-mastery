## POST 2: 10-Slide Instagram / Facebook Carousel: "How to Read Hangul in 10 Minutes"
**Cover Slide Headline:** Stop Saying Korean is Hard 🛑 How to Read Hangul in 10 Minutes (Swipe $\rightarrow$)

* **Slide 1 (Cover):** How to Read Korean (Hangul) in 10 Minutes. Built scientifically in 1443 by King Sejong.
* **Slide 2 (The Vowels):**
  - ㅏ = Open "Ah" (like Father)
  - ㅗ = Rounded "Oh" (like Boat)
  - ㅜ = Tight "Oo" (like Moon)
  - ㅣ = Smiling "Ee" (like See)
* **Slide 3 (The Consonants):**
  - ㄱ = G / K sound
  - ㄴ = N sound (tongue touches upper gum)
  - ㅁ = M sound (mouth closed like a box)
  - ㅅ = S sound
* **Slide 4 (The Block Rule):** Korean letters NEVER sit in a straight line. They snap together into 2-letter or 3-letter square blocks:
  - Initial Consonant + Medial Vowel (+ Final Consonant / Batchim).
* **Slide 5 (Example 1):**
  - ㄱ (G) + ㅏ (A) = 가 (Ga)
  - ㄴ (N) + ㅏ (A) = 나 (Na)
  - 가나 = "Gana" (Ghana)!
* **Slide 6 (Example 2 with 3 letters):**
  - ㅎ (H) + ㅏ (A) + ㄴ (N) = 한 (Han)
  - ㄱ (G) + ㅜ (U) + ㄱ (K) = 국 (Guk)
  - 한국 (Han-guk) = South Korea! 🇰🇷
* **Slide 7 (Example 3 - Your favorite word):**
  - ㅁ (M) + ㅜ (U) + ㄹ (L) = 물 (Mul) = Water!
  - ㅂ (B) + ㅏ (A) + ㅂ (P) = 밥 (Bap) = Cooked Rice / Food!
* **Slide 8 (The Two Numbers):**
  - Sino-Korean (일, 이, 삼): Use for MONEY and DATES.
  - Native Korean (하나, 둘, 셋): Use for PEOPLE and COUNTING OBJECTS.
