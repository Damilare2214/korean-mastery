## 2. The 4 High-Converting Audience Personas in Nigeria

### Persona 1: The EPS-TOPIK Jobseeker (*The Highest LTV / Easiest ₦2,000 Conversion*)
* **Who they are:** Ambitious young men and women aged 20–38 seeking legitimate legal relocation and employment in South Korea under the official government **Employment Permit System (E-9 Visa)**.
* **Core Pain Point:** Private language centres in Lagos and Abuja charge ₦50,000–₦150,000 for coaching. Traveling to physical classes is impossible for people living outside state capitals.
* **The Irresistible Angle:** *"South Korea factory workers earn ₩2,060,000+ per month (~₦2,300,000+ NGN). The mandatory EPS-TOPIK test is 40 multiple-choice questions. Skip expensive brokers — start learning today for ₦0 and unlock full vocational prep for ₦2,000."*
* **Where to reach them:** TikTok, Nairaland (Travel & Jobs boards), Facebook EPS-TOPIK groups, WhatsApp job seeker groups.

### Persona 2: The K-Drama & K-Pop Superfan (*The Highest Volume / Fast ₦500 Conversion*)
* **Who they are:** Gen Z & Millennials (65%+ female) who spend hours watching Korean dramas on Netflix Nigeria, webtoons, and listening to BTS, BLACKPINK, NewJeans, and Stray Kids.
* **Core Pain Point:** Tired of lagging English subtitles, feeling left out of Korean cultural references, not understanding nuances like *Oppa, Unnie, Sunbae*, or unable to sing along accurately.
* **The Irresistible Angle:** *"Hangul was invented in 1443 so anyone can learn it in one afternoon. Learn to read Korean script today for free, and order food like a Seoul local for only ₦500."*
* **Where to reach them:** TikTok (K-drama clips, POV videos), Instagram Reels, X (Twitter) K-pop Stan accounts, WhatsApp Statuses.

### Persona 3: The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) Hunter (*The High-Intent Academic*)
* **Who they are:** High-achieving undergraduates and graduates seeking fully funded university education in South Korea (100% tuition paid + monthly living stipend + flights).
* **Core Pain Point:** Thousands apply through the Korean Cultural Centre Nigeria (KCCN) in Abuja; without Korean language certification, standing out is nearly impossible.
* **The Irresistible Angle:** *"Boost your GKS Embassy Track application with a verified digital Certificate of Completion in Korean language."*
* **Where to reach them:** LinkedIn, Nairaland Education board, campus WhatsApp groups, KCCN event channels.

### Persona 4: The Corporate Professional & Conglomerate Contractor
* **Who they are:** Engineers, logistics coordinators, supply chain specialists, and corporate executives working with Daewoo E&C (NLNG Train 7), Samsung Heavy Industries (SHI-MCI), LG Electronics, and Hyundai.
* **Core Pain Point:** Communication barriers with Korean expatriate managers and unfamiliarity with Korean corporate etiquette (*Nunchi*, bowing hierarchy, business card rituals).
* **The Irresistible Angle:** *"Master Korean corporate etiquette, two-hand rules, and workplace phrases to accelerate promotions and contracts."*
* **Where to reach them:** LinkedIn, corporate trade networks, Port Harcourt / Lagos professional circles.

## 5. Paystack & Safety Best Practices for Nigeria

1. **Keep "Pass Transaction Fees to Customer" Turned OFF** in the Paystack Dashboard.
   * *Why:* If turned on, a ₦500 course will charge ₦507.62, which fails strict amount verification algorithms and locks students out.
2. **Automated Self-Healing Reconciliation:**
   * Every time `/api/auth/me` is called (on every login or page refresh), the system cross-checks any pending references against Paystack, guaranteeing access even if the user accidentally closed their browser during bank transfer.
3. **Transparent Trust Signals:**
   * Place Paystack and SSL badges prominently near the checkout button.
   * Emphasize: *"One-time payment in Naira. No subscriptions. Lifetime access."*
