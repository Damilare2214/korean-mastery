Why Korean Language is Your Highest-ROI Skill in Nigeria

South Korea is actively recruiting skilled and non-professional personnel through official bilateral government agreements. Language proficiency is the single gatekeeper.

EPS E-9 Work Visas

The Korean government's Employment Permit System matches foreign workers with manufacturing, agriculture, and construction companies. Guaranteed minimum wage is ₩2.06M+ (~₦2.3M/month). Passing EPS-TOPIK is mandatory to enter the HRD roster.

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)

Fully funded bachelor's, master's, and PhD programs offered through the Korean Cultural Centre Nigeria (KCCN) in Abuja. 100% free tuition, monthly stipends, and roundtrip flight. Korean ability gives you huge competitive points.

South Korean multinational giants operate major engineering and commercial operations in Lagos, Bonny Island, and Abuja. Staff with Korean conversational ability and business etiquette stand out for promotions and leadership.
